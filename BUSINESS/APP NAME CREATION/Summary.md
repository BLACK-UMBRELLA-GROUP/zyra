============================================================
BRAND NAMING PRINCIPLES
============================================================

The official app name should:
• Be one word.
• Be memorable after hearing it once.
• Be simple to pronounce internationally.
• Feel premium.
• Scale into a large global company.
• Build trust.
• Work across logos, websites, social media and app stores.
• Avoid sounding like a typical dating app.

Evaluation Criteria
Every future name should be judged against:
1. Memorability
2. Pronunciation
3. Simplicity
4. Premium feel
5. Trustworthiness
6. Brand scalability
7. Domain availability
8. Social username availability
9. App Store suitability
10. Trademark availability
