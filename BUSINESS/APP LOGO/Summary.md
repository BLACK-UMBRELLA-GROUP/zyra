------------------------------------------------------------
BRAND DIRECTION
------------------------------------------------------------

• Modern
• Premium
• Trustworthy
• Minimal
• Memorable
• Mobile-first

------------------------------------------------------------
DESIGN GOALS
------------------------------------------------------------

• Create a logo that is instantly recognisable.
• The logo should work without displaying the name "ZYRA".
• The logo should be simple enough to function as an app icon.
• The design should feel like a modern technology company
  rather than a traditional marriage service.

------------------------------------------------------------
APPROVED DESIGN DIRECTION
------------------------------------------------------------

• A standalone icon with no text.
• Purple is the primary brand colour.
• Blue and pink may be used as supporting colours.
• Soft, rounded, premium appearance.
• Clean and minimal.
• Timeless rather than trendy.

------------------------------------------------------------
CURRENT LEADING LOGO CONCEPT
------------------------------------------------------------

• Two interlocking wedding bands.
• One blue ring.
• One rose-gold/pink ring.
• The rings are displayed directly on the purple brand background.
• Remove the white background entirely.
• The rings themselves become the logo.

------------------------------------------------------------
AVOID
------------------------------------------------------------

• Text inside the logo.
• Hearts.
• Human figures.
• Generic dating icons.
• Mosque silhouettes.
• Overly complex details.

------------------------------------------------------------
IMPLEMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
------------------------------------------------------------

• Purple fills the complete icon background.
• No white background behind the rings.
• The rings remain the primary focal point.
• Maintain a premium appearance.
• Soft lighting.
• Subtle 3D depth.
• High recognisability at small icon sizes.

------------------------------------------------------------
REQUIRED EXPORTS
------------------------------------------------------------

• App Icon
• Transparent PNG
• SVG Vector
• Website Logo
• Light Mode Version
• Dark Mode Version
• App Store Assets
