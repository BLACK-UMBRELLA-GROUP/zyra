- **Referenced By:** Website, campaign landing pages and social-channel operations.

## SUMMARY

The ZYRA Marketing System is an approval-led AI social-media team for TikTok, Instagram and Facebook, with the existing website as the destination.

Codex is the main operator and leads marketing strategy, content, product accuracy, channel administration, security coordination, compliance records and spending controls. ChatGPT is bounded support only. The Founder approves every public item, sensitive reply, campaign and budget.

The system contains research, strategy, content production, brand protection, an approval centre, publishing, community-response drafting, and analytics/memory. Sensitive or high-risk situations never receive an automatic public response.

The initial pillars are ZYRA's difference from dating apps, Guardian/family involvement, marriage-first introductions, safety/trust/privacy, compatibility, Seeker advice, Guardian/parent advice, the Founder story, product demonstrations, and pre-launch testing.

The command centre is mounted inside AIOS at `/marketing/`. It includes a collection-only Inspiration inbox: saved video links are retained in arrival order and exact repeat URLs are marked, but nothing is analysed or executed until the Founder ends collection. It cannot access social accounts, publish, reply publicly or spend money. Three sample ideas are drafts only. Manual Founder-approved publishing precedes scheduling, analytics integrations and cautious automation.

Website work is separate and requires an explicit instruction; the existing website was not changed by the approved marketing-team implementation.

Governing principle: **AI prepares and learns; the Founder controls what represents ZYRA publicly.**
