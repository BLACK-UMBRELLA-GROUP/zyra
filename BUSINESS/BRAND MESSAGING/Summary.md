FEATURE NAME
BRAND MESSAGING
Group: 7 — UI/UX Design

============================================================
SUMMARY
============================================================

Purpose:
Define the official ZYRA brand identity, including logo
direction, tagline, splash screen branding, and brand
positioning.

Owns:
• Official brand tagline
• Splash screen branding
• Brand positioning
• Brand guidelines

Note:
• Official app name is owned by BUSINESS/APP NAME CREATION.

------------------------------------------------------------
APPROVED TAGLINE
------------------------------------------------------------

Connecting Through Trust

Purpose:
The tagline represents the entire ZYRA ecosystem rather than
a single user type. It reflects the relationship between
Seekers, Guardians, families, and the trust-based marriage journey.

------------------------------------------------------------
BRAND GOALS
------------------------------------------------------------

• Premium
• Modern
• Memorable
• Trust-focused
• Marriage-first
• Neutral (not Seeker-only or Guardian-only)

------------------------------------------------------------
BRAND DIRECTION
------------------------------------------------------------

• ZYRA is positioned as a trusted marriage platform rather
  than a dating app.
• Trust, guidance, and meaningful connections are core brand
  values.
• The branding should communicate professionalism, simplicity,
  and long-term commitment.

------------------------------------------------------------
BRAND PRINCIPLES
------------------------------------------------------------

• Trust
• Guidance
• Family involvement
• Serious marriage
• Professional experience

------------------------------------------------------------
SPLASH SCREEN
------------------------------------------------------------

Display:
ZYRA
Connecting Through Trust

Implementation:
• Display the ZYRA logo prominently.
• Display "Connecting Through Trust" beneath the logo.
• Keep the layout minimal, centred, and elegant.
• Maintain consistent branding across the app, website,
  and marketing.
