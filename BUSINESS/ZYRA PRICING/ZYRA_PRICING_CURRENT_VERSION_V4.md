# ZYRA — Membership and Pricing

**Version:** V4
**Status:** CURRENT_VERSION — Founder-ratified model; payment activation not complete
**Date:** 2026-09-18
**Owner / approval authority:** Founder
**Supersedes:** ZYRA_PRICING_CURRENT_VERSION_V3.md
**Decision:** DR-ZYRA-234
**Implementation action:** OA-ZYRA-253

## 1. Approved membership structure

| Membership | Price | Core proposition |
|---|---:|---|
| **ZYRA One — Seeker** | **£29.99/month** | Complete premium Seeker and matching experience. |
| **ZYRA One — Guardian** | **£19.99/month** | Active Guardian participation across permitted linked-Seeker Journeys. |
| **Guardian Free** | **Free** | View the linked Seeker's Journey only. |
| **ZYRA Family** | Unchanged | Existing Family pricing and terms are outside this repricing and remain unchanged. |

The former weekly plans are retired from the current catalogue. The former
combined ZYRA One membership, included Guardian allocation and Additional
Guardian subscription product are superseded. **Additional Guardian** remains
a relationship/authority role; it is not a subscription product name.

Compatibility Unlock and Unlock Plus remain separate optional purchases at
their existing prices: £1.99 each or £7.99 for five, and £2.99 each or £9.99
for five respectively. This decision does not activate their checkout.

## 2. Guardian capability boundary

**Guardian Free observes.** A free Guardian may accept and retain a valid
Guardian relationship and view the linked Seeker's Journey. Guardian Free has
no Journey commenting, no Journey-conversation entry and no Guardian Network
page.

**ZYRA One — Guardian participates.** Subject to the existing accepted-link,
Seeker-permission, Journey privacy and Main-Guardian authority rules, the paid
Guardian experience includes:

- viewing and commenting across the linked Seeker's Journey;
- entering permitted Journey conversations;
- using Guardian Network to discover Seekers and make recommendations.

"Enter conversations" does not silently authorise message sending. The current
Journey conversation surface is read-only; any Guardian message-sending model
requires its own explicit product, privacy and server-authority decision.
Journey-wide Guardian comments are an approved product capability but are not
yet backed by a production comment service.

Invitation acceptance, consent decisions, reporting, blocking, privacy and
essential safety controls remain available without payment. Payment never
creates a Guardian link, promotes a Guardian to Main, grants access to an
unlinked Seeker or bypasses romantic exclusions.

## 3. Trial and billing boundary

The previously approved planned fourteen-day trial is retained for
**ZYRA One — Seeker** only. A Guardian trial has not been approved. No weekly,
annual or Guardian-trial offer may be inferred.

The app still has no live StoreKit/Play purchase adapter, verified receipt
pipeline or paid-access activation. Catalogue presentation is not entitlement
proof. The legacy database plan identifiers remain compatibility internals and
must not dictate member-facing product names.

Before live billing, the Guardian entitlement resolver must stop treating a
linked Seeker's paid plan as proof of the Guardian's own ZYRA One — Guardian
membership. Paid comment, conversation and Network authority must be enforced
server-side; client navigation hiding is not a security boundary.

## 4. Continuity and unchanged rules

- ZYRA Family is unchanged by this decision.
- A subscription change never deletes Guardian relationships, Journeys,
  messages, history or configuration.
- Main/Additional Guardian authority remains separate from membership.
- Seeker-controlled per-Journey Additional-Guardian inclusion remains in
  force.
- Compatibility purchases and already-held rights are unchanged.
- No production migration, payment product or TestFlight build is authorised
  solely by this pricing decision.
