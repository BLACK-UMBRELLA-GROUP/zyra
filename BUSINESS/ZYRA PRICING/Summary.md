TERMINOLOGY
------------------------------------------------------------

| Term                 | Meaning |
|----------------------|---------|
| Seeker               | Person looking for marriage. |
| Wali                 | Person supervising or representing a Seeker. |
| Seeker Account       | Account used by a Seeker to search for marriage. |
| Wali Account         | Account used by a Wali to supervise one or more Seekers. |
| Wali Relationship    | The connection between a Seeker and a Wali. |
| Wali Subscription    | The subscription purchased for a Wali Relationship, giving that Wali the selected plan (Basic, Pro or Premium) for that Seeker. |

| Profile Unlock       | Optional purchase that unlocks additional profile information. Pricing only is defined here. Full behaviour is documented in APP/Premium/PROFILE UNLOCKS. |
| Potentials           | People being considered for marriage through ZYRA. Users may view, like, match with, save, discuss or progress with Potentials. They remain Potentials until the marriage journey concludes. |

FOUNDER & EARLY-ADOPTER WALI DISCOUNT RULES
------------------------------------------------------------

The following rules apply only to Founder and Early-Adopter offers.

• Discounts belong to the Seeker Account.
• Each eligible Seeker Account receives:
  • 1 Free Wali Pro Subscription
  • Up to 3 Discounted Wali Subscriptions
• The included Free Wali Pro Subscription does not count
  towards the 3 discounted Wali Subscription limit.
• Discount limits apply per Seeker Account.
• Discount limits do not apply per Wali Account or household.

------------------------------------------------------------
WALI RELATIONSHIP RULES
------------------------------------------------------------

• Every Wali requires a Wali Account.
• Every Seeker–Wali Relationship requires its own Wali Subscription.
• Multiple Walis may supervise the same Seeker.
• A Wali may supervise multiple verified Seekers.
• Pricing remains identical regardless of who pays.

------------------------------------------------------------
WALI BILLING STRUCTURE
------------------------------------------------------------

Each Wali has:
• One Login
• One Dashboard
• One Billing Account
• One Invoice

Each Seeker–Wali Relationship appears as its own subscription
within that single billing account.

Additional subscriptions:
• Added immediately.
• Charged on a prorated basis.
• Automatically aligned with the existing renewal date.
• Renew together on one invoice.

Example 1 — Additional Seeker Relationship

Month 1
| Relationship  | Plan                 |
|---------------|----------------------|
| Dad → Son A   | Wali Premium (£9.99) |
Renewal Date: 1 July

20 Days Later
| Relationship  | Plan                 |
|---------------|----------------------|
| Dad → Son B   | Wali Premium (£9.99) |

System Behaviour:
• Calculates the remaining days until 1 July.
• Charges only the prorated amount.
• Adds Son B to Dad's existing billing account.

Next Renewal
| Relationship | Monthly |
|--------------|--------:|
| Son A        | £9.99   |
| Son B        | £9.99   |
Total Monthly Bill = £19.98

Example 2 — Additional Wali Relationship
If a Seeker later adds another Wali:
• New Wali Subscription created immediately.
• First payment prorated.
• Subscription aligned with existing billing cycle.
• Future renewals appear on the same invoice.

------------------------------------------------------------
PAYMENT & OWNERSHIP RULES
------------------------------------------------------------

A Seeker may pay for:
• Their own Seeker Account
• One or more Wali Accounts
• Another verified Seeker Account

A Wali may pay for:
• Their own Wali Account
• One or more Seeker Accounts
• One or more Wali Accounts

Rules
• Payment ownership never determines account ownership.
• Every account belongs to the verified individual using it.
• Multiple paid accounts are permitted only when every account
  belongs to a different verified individual.

Verification Rules
• Additional Seeker Accounts must belong to different verified individuals.
• Additional Wali Accounts must belong to different verified individuals.
• One individual cannot operate multiple Seeker Accounts for themselves.
• One individual cannot operate multiple Wali Accounts for themselves.
• Verification processes prevent duplicate-account abuse.
