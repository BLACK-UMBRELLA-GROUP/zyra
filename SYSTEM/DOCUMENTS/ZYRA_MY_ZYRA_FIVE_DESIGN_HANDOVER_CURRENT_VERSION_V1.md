## 3. The five options

All options show only eligible content, route to the same owner pages, cap
recent changes at three and editorial at one, and never implement owner actions.
The following differences are presentation, not five different business rules.

| Design / intended experience | Concept and reading order | Quiet / busy handling | Weakness or trade-off |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 · Personal Briefing** — a warm personal home | Editorial heading → unboxed leading obligation → compact further obligations → status margin note → light recent rows → company letter. Typography rather than cards establishes importance. | Quiet is an intentional short briefing. Busy keeps one typographic lead and lighter supporting rows. | Can be vertically longer; the personalised opening uses some first-screen space. |
| **2 · Focus + Timeline** — immediate direction and continuity | One contained focus → compact conditions → continuous recent-change rail → final editorial. Insights use an evidence-led reading stream. | Quiet has no empty timeline or focus box. Busy has one obvious focus, not equal cards. | Timeline indentation reduces text width; long content requires scrolling. |
| **3 · Layered Home** — reassuring spatial hierarchy | Foreground priority → recessed context → darker movement layer → separate editorial. Continuous tonal layers replace unrelated cards. | Quiet has one complete, spacious surface; absent layers disappear. Busy creates depth without overlapping text or content clipping. | More visual weight and a longer vertical stack than the editorial options. |
| **4 · Compact Bento** — a quick modular overview | Full-width priority → unequal status/movement modules → editorial. Insights lead with one full-width finding then supporting modules. | No reserved grid cells. At ≥360px content width and ≤1.15× text, secondary modules use a 56/44 pairing; narrow/larger-text layouts become one column. | Denser wide-screen reading; unequal content heights can leave breathing room under the shorter module. |
| **5 · Quiet Index** — a deliberately minimal reading surface | Exposed leading obligation and current conditions → named change/editorial entries with explicit Read details controls. Insights expose one lead, then an index. | Quiet is plain and complete. Busy keeps secondary reading collapsed, never hiding obligations or status. | Secondary details take an extra tap; less immediate detail than the other four. |

**Codex preference: Personal Briefing.** It is the clearest departure from the
old card collection while preserving readable priority and context. Focus +
Timeline is the strongest alternative if the Founder prefers a more explicit
current-focus anchor. This preference is not approval or promotion.

## 6. Quiet, busy and failure states

- Quiet Now is shown only when the relevant health is complete and there are
  no actions, statuses, changes or editorial. It says nothing needs attention;
  it adds no behavioural suggestion, inventory or fake statistic.
- One action, one status, three changes or editorial-only pages render their
  real subset without blank section headings or placeholder cells.
- Busy pages lead with one obligation, show two additional ones compactly,
  and expose the rest through the shared more-obligations control. Source
  obligations remain uncapped; only initial display is bounded.
- Loading and failed states cannot become all-clear. Failure is visibly
  unavailable with the existing retry callback, including Insights failure.
- Retained Updates display **Last known · refresh needed**, and every retained
  row is independently labelled **Last known · not current**. Quiet Index's
  collapsed rows retain this provenance, date and nullable-unread treatment.
- Production/current-source Insights remain honestly empty; failed or loading
  Insight sources suppress fixture findings instead of presenting them fresh.
- The fixed failure fixtures deliberately remain fixed on retry for review;
  existing-source retry uses the real existing loader.
