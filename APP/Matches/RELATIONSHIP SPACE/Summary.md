FEATURE NAME
RELATIONSHIP SPACE
Group: 4 — Page Responsibilities

============================================================
SUMMARY
============================================================

Purpose:
The permanent home of a relationship between two matched
users. Accessed from the Matches page. Contains everything
related to that specific Journey.

Previously referred to as "Timeline" — Relationship Space
is the current working name.

------------------------------------------------------------
CONTENTS
------------------------------------------------------------

The Relationship Space contains:
• Timeline
• Active Journey stage
• Wali
• Profile
• Chat (when unlocked)

------------------------------------------------------------
ENTRY POINT
------------------------------------------------------------

• Accessed via the arrow on a collapsed match card,
  or via the expanded match card entry point.
• Each match has its own Relationship Space.
• Users may enter the Relationship Space for any match,
  even if that match does not have the active conversation.

------------------------------------------------------------
ACTIVE CONVERSATION RULE
------------------------------------------------------------

Only one active conversation is permitted across all
Relationship Spaces.

If another relationship currently holds the active
conversation:
• Timeline remains viewable.
• Relationship progress remains viewable.
• Chat cannot continue until the current active
  conversation ends or the Journey changes state.

------------------------------------------------------------
TIMELINE
------------------------------------------------------------

• Records the history of the relationship.
• Survives after a Journey ends.
• Conversation status and Timeline are separate concepts.
• May record "Journey Ended" as the final milestone
  (future discussion).

------------------------------------------------------------
DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
------------------------------------------------------------

• Avoid adding complexity until the interface is built
  and tested visually.
• The simplest version first.
• Each component (Timeline, Chat, Profile, Wali) to be
  designed and reviewed independently.
