**Large** means more than seven million monthly active UK users: preceding six-month mean, or shorter operating history. Once exceeded, the threshold continues to apply until the mean has been at or below seven million for six continuous months. Paid-account or storage counts are not the defined usage metric. ICU §§5.7–5.10; PCU §§5.6–5.9.
