## 1. Conclusion first

| Question | Fable's view | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Is ZYRA a **user-to-user service** under the Act (s.3)? | **Yes.** Members upload profiles, photos and messages that other members (Seekers, Guardians, the Guardian Circle) encounter. Private messaging between users counts | High - verify |
| Does it have **links with the UK** (s.4)? | **Yes.** UK company, UK members, UK launch | High |
| Is it exempt? | **No obvious exemption.** It is not a one-to-one live-voice service, an email/SMS service, an internal business tool, or a comments-only service; a "limited functionality" exemption does not fit because members post free text and photos | Medium - solicitor to confirm |
| Size category | **Small, low-risk-leaning** service at launch (well under Ofcom's "large" thresholds of 7 million UK monthly users); but matching, private messaging and photo sharing raise its **risk profile** for certain harms (grooming is unlikely given 18+ with identity checks; harassment, intimate-image abuse and fraud are plausible) | Medium - verify Ofcom thresholds |
| Is it "likely to be accessed by children"? | **Should not be**, because every member passes a document-based identity check with age extraction before the app opens (DR-ZYRA-221; DOC-ZYRA-0296 §1 step 7) and the store rating is 18+. But the production switch is **off** today (DOC-ZYRA-0296 §3 item 2), so the children's access assessment cannot be answered "no" until it is on | High once the switch is on |
| Bottom line | **ZYRA must do the illegal-content risk assessment, keep it current, apply the relevant Ofcom illegal-harms code measures for a small service, run a reporting and complaints process, and have clear terms - all before launch** | — |

For a small service Ofcom's code measures include: a named senior person accountable for compliance; content moderation that reviews and removes illegal content; easy reporting; a complaints process; terms that say what is not allowed; keeping records | Reporting and blocking are live; **review process missing** (DR-ZYRA-287 to build - DOC-ZYRA-0332); terms not yet published (DOC-ZYRA-0330) | Founder (accountable person); Fable; Codex (AIOS page) |
