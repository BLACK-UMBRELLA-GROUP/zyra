## 1. Founder verdict

**Prepare privately now, but do not start a substantial public campaign or paid advertising yet.** This is my risk recommendation, not a claim that incorporation or a granted trademark is legally compulsory before every promotional post.

The priority is not opening every possible social account. It is:

1. Obtain a professional clearance opinion for **ZYRA**, including **ZYRA Connect** and relevant similar names.
2. Decide the actual legal operator and who will own the brand and accounts.
3. Secure the small set of important domains/handles and complete account recovery/security checks.
4. Complete privacy arrangements for the website, enquiries and any launch mailing list.
5. Reconcile marketing claims, prices and product availability; check each advertising platform’s eligibility rules.
6. Before real-member testing or launch, finish the separate app privacy, safety and security acceptance work.

**Your name is already exposed:** the website is publicly accessible and social accounts have been created. That does not mean somebody has stolen it. It means future protection cannot depend on secrecy alone.

The name is **not cleared**. Existing ZYRA software and company uses, plus trademark leads, mean the material risk is both someone copying you **and an earlier rights-holder challenging you**. No recommendation here means you must rename; the search is a reason for specialist clearance before more investment.

## 3. Protecting the name

### Different assets protect different things

| Asset | What it does | What it does not do |
|---|---|---|
| Registered company name | Identifies a legal company and is subject to Companies House naming rules. | Does not give exclusive trademark rights to ZYRA. |
| Registered trademark | Protects the registered sign for its covered goods/services and territory, subject to earlier rights and applicable law. | Does not give worldwide ownership of a word or automatic entitlement to every matching domain/handle. |
| Domain | Controls that internet address while validly held and renewed. | Does not clear the brand or prevent another business using a similar name elsewhere. |
| Social username | Holds an account identifier under the platform’s rules. | Does not substitute for trademark rights or guarantee permanent retention. |
| Copyright and contracts | Can protect qualifying code, artwork and text and establish who owns/licences them. | Do not generally give a monopoly over the business idea or product concept. |

UKIPO explicitly distinguishes company, domain and trademark protection. Earlier legitimate domains do not become yours merely because you later register a mark. [UKIPO guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/unacceptable-trade-marks)

### Recommended protection route

- Ask a UK trademark attorney for a written clearance opinion on the actual **ZYRA** brand, **ZYRA Connect**, relevant sound/spelling variants, and the leads above. Adding “Connect” or using a logo must not be assumed to cure a conflict.
- Ask about a **word mark** for the name actually used, with logo protection considered separately. Scope Class **45** for relevant matchmaking/marriage/social-networking services; Class **9** for the downloadable app; assess **42** for relevant hosted software and **38** for communications services actually supplied. Do not mechanically buy every class. [WIPO classification](https://nclpub.wipo.int/enfr/pdf-download.pdf?dateInForce=20260101&lang=enfr&tab=class_headings)
- Resolve applicant ownership. A person can apply in their own legal name before incorporation; a nonexistent company cannot be the applicant. Later assignment needs proper documentation. The earlier incorporate-first choice can be retained or changed by the Founder—it is a choice, not a universal legal prerequisite. [UKIPO applicant rules](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/trade-marks-manual/new-applications)
- After clearance, coordinate important domain/handle reservations and filing closely. UKIPO warns that application details become public and may expose a desired domain to others. Secure approved priority assets before that disclosure where feasible. [Application guidance](https://www.gov.uk/how-to-register-a-trade-mark/start-your-application)
- Filing is not a granted registration. Agree with the attorney how much promotion to undertake while examination/opposition remains open. Use no registered-mark claim or ® before it is justified. UK protection is not worldwide; diary an international strategy decision if expansion is intended. [UK registration](https://www.gov.uk/how-to-register-a-trade-mark), [overseas protection](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/protecting-your-trade-mark-abroad)
- Preserve dated design/source files, brand-use evidence, invoices and agreements. Use confidentiality terms for genuinely confidential material shared with contractors, not as an assumed shield over material already published. Commissioned work is not automatically owned outright just because it was paid for. Obtain appropriate written assignments/licences and review third-party/AI-asset rights. [Copyright ownership](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/ownership-of-copyright-works), [assignments](https://www.gov.uk/copyright/license-and-sell-your-copyright)

## 4. Company and legal ownership

**Do not rush into a name merely to feel protected.** The app may be branded ZYRA while its legal operator has a different cleared company name. Your current “ZYRA Connect” preference and older Black Umbrella Group structure proposal need one explicit Founder decision, assisted by an accountant/solicitor where necessary.

Before forming a company, settle its proposed legal name, ownership/directors/PSCs, registered-office/service address, activities and basic records. Complete the applicable identity-verification steps. Use an appropriate address arrangement if home-address exposure is a concern; do not casually publish your home address. Current digital incorporation is £100. A company also brings ongoing filing/accounting duties, so extra dormant or holding companies are not free brand protection. [Company setup](https://www.gov.uk/set-up-limited-company), [registration](https://www.gov.uk/limited-company-formation/register-your-company), [identity verification](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/verifying-your-identity-for-companies-house)

Then record the exact legal owner of domains, marks, code, app-store/developer accounts and social business assets. Arrange the necessary founder-to-company IP assignment and vendor/account transfers rather than assuming incorporation transfers everything automatically. Separate company money and records when the company exists.

**Privacy duties do not wait for incorporation.** Identify who actually controls website/enquiry/member data now. If it is you personally trading as ZYRA, the notice must accurately reflect that pending a properly managed transition. Do not name an unformed “ZYRA Ltd” as controller. [Trading names](https://www.gov.uk/become-sole-trader/choose-your-business-name), [ICO introduction](https://ico.org.uk/media/for-organisations/guide-to-data-protection/introduction-to-dpa-2018-1-0.pdf)
