## 4. Jurisdiction and structure options — no route selected

UK law permits sole trading as well as incorporation; sole trading has personal
liability. Do not claim every business must be incorporated before any public
statement. Separately, Apple's sensitive-information submission policy can
make the relevant legal-entity route material to this product. Founder must
reconcile business structure with the intended store service, not choose it
by a dashboard label. [UK sole-trader guidance](https://www.gov.uk/become-sole-trader/what-sole-trader),
[Apple review §5.1.1(ix)](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/).

For a UK company, settle legal name, appropriate registered office, directors,
shareholders/PSC, activity code and verification requirements before filing.
The certificate/number prove incorporation; a prepared form does not. Do not
promise a same-day bank opening or infer a universal verification delay.
Address/privacy and legal-name checks need their own decisions. Incorporation
does not by itself clear a trademark. [Companies House registration](https://www.gov.uk/limited-company-formation/register-your-company),
[company-name rules](https://www.gov.uk/limited-company-formation/choose-company-name).

The Founder's Saudi registration enquiry is not a country, eligibility or
incorporation decision. Current MISA guidance describes investment registration
for eligible entities/foreign companies and Premium Residency individuals,
with activity-dependent conditions and routes. Nationality/residency, actual
app activity, ownership and available entity evidence have not been established
here; **Saudi route HELD**, with no application begun. An indexed Ministry of
Commerce page using older “investment license” wording was not retrievable;
it is not relied on to determine current eligibility. [MISA FAQ, modified
24 June 2026](https://misa.gov.sa/frequently-asked-questions/).

Incorporation country, operator establishment, user residence, marketing target,
storefront availability, tax nexus and supplier data location are different
questions. Registering in one country does not clear launch in every other.
No new statutory/local-adviser gate is invented; unresolved applicable duties
remain explicit decisions/evidence requirements under the in-house route.
