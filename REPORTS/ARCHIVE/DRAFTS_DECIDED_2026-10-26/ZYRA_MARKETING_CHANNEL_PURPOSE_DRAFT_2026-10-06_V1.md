## 3. Three reusable editorial types, not three posts everywhere

These are optional packaging groups inside V4's ten pillars, not replacement
policy or an approved campaign:

| Type | Useful private brief | Reuse pattern | Product-truth boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Helpful | A general, carefully sourced question about expectations or family involvement | Vertical explanation → carousel → one Pin; short text summary if useful | No personalised advice, religious ruling, asserted clinical benefit or implied live ZYRA feature. Expert/legal/religious review where the actual content needs it |
| Process / product truth | Explain one intended role or permission boundary, explicitly as planned | Qualified synthetic demo or diagram → plain-language carousel → candidate FAQ | C01/C07/C09/C10 map in §5. Planned intent must not become present-tense operational assurance |
| Conversation / building | A respectful open question, or a factual non-sensitive progress update | Threads/Facebook question → separately adapted X text; Reddit rebuilt if appropriate | No private member story, invented testimonial, marriage outcome, user count or launch date. C18/C17 restrictions apply |

“Trust” is a brand value, not a safety certification. Use calm, clear,
marriage-first language and **ZYRA** as the default product name. Avoid dating/
swipe-game positioning, superiority language, fake urgency and generic AI hype.
Brand Guidelines V1 supplies non-conflicting voice only: its historical visual,
font/logo/status statements do not override newer app/website evidence. No new
visual design, formal legal-operator name or registration assertion is made here.

V4 permits a maximum of **three distinct master ideas per Europe/London day**,
with additional variants of the same idea. It neither mandates one daily post
nor authorises three posts on every platform. Propose measuring one master idea
on a chosen content day first; increase only if production, Founder review and
community handling fit capacity. This is not a scheduled job or calendar entry.
No quota applies to Reddit or enquiry replies.

### Reuse rules

1. Keep one clean, owned/licensed source video with readable captions. Export
   a destination-specific version; check crop, safe areas, accessibility, cover,
   caption, rights, context and CTA separately. Cross-platform access never proves
   that music or third-party footage is licensed on every destination.
2. Reuse the idea and research across video, carousel and text, not a badly fitted
   finished file. Retain claim qualifications in image text, voiceover and caption.
3. Reddit is a bespoke community contribution, not a distribution endpoint.
   WhatsApp is a possible staffed enquiry service, not assumed bulk marketing.
4. Do not analyse or copy the saved inspiration videos in collection-only mode.
   The existing media manifest is an inventory with holds, not clearance to use
   any photograph, font, screenshot, music or synthetic person publicly.
