## Purpose and test boundary

This plan turns the eight scenarios in the existing safety-response draft §8
into proposed synthetic inputs, decisions and evidence. It is a paper test
specification, not a rehearsal or evidence that a report will reach a human.
An isolated authorised test, real appointed people and later acceptance are
separate steps. Today's documents alone cannot satisfy those gates.

Use only invented adult labels SIM-SEEKER-A, SIM-SEEKER-B, SIM-GUARDIAN-C,
SIM-PAYER-D and SIM-REPORTER-E; harmless text; fabricated case references; and
synthetic timestamps. No real account, contact address, photograph, intimate
material, identity document, member message or signed URL is needed. A child
concern is an invented text description, never an image or a real minor fixture.

Safety lead, duty responder, backup, appeal reviewer and privacy lead are **roles
to be appointed**, not people appointed here. Codex is not the duty responder.
No messages, submissions, content restrictions, deletion or provider calls may
be sent/performed from this specification.

## Common acceptance record

For each later authorised scenario, record: exact candidate/policy versions and
hashes; isolated environment and permitted actions; synthetic input and original
timestamps; role/access matrix; expected decisions; actual events with actor and
time; duplicate/retry behaviour; deadline reasoning; positive and denial evidence;
failure/rollback result; unresolved actions; and separate Founder acceptance.

Distinguish received, assigned, reviewed, action taken and closed. A label changes
only when its event occurs. Record NOT RUN until actual execution; then PASS,
FAIL or BLOCKED with evidence. An unfilled human role, missing authority or absent
delivery proof is BLOCKED, not a simulated PASS. An allegation is not a guilt
finding. A payer/Guardian relationship is not authority to receive another adult's
private data.

## Scenario 1 Report assignment and status

**Status: NOT RUN.** Input: SIM-REPORTER-E submits harmless report CASE-01 about
SIM-SEEKER-B, then retries the same submission after a synthetic timeout.
Expected: one logical case and original receipt time; an appointed responder
actually receives and acknowledges assignment in a later isolated rehearsal;
status reflects real events. Denial: reporter and unrelated member cannot read
internal notes or assign/close a case. Required evidence: idempotence, role denials,
assignment delivery, audit continuity and truthful reporter status. Stop on
duplicate cases, unauthorised notes, false review status or unstaffed delivery.
No numerical service response promise is selected.

## Scenario 2 Report without an active membership

**Status: NOT RUN.** Input: an invented person represented by SIM-REPORTER-E cannot
sign in and reports an invented NCII concern without supplying media. Repeat as
a deactivated account and a non-member. Expected: the approved external intake
route can receive the concern without login/payment or forced harmful evidence;
appointed human assesses urgency and safe contact. Denial: no notification to the
reported person or Guardian by default; no demand to forward intimate files.
Required evidence: route receipt, restricted case, safe contact decision and
handoff. Stop if access/payment blocks reporting or the route is unmonitored.

## Scenario 3 Child concern or immediate danger

**Status: NOT RUN.** Input: harmless fabricated text describing uncertain age or
credible imminent danger; no real child identity or image. Expected: urgent
trained-human branch; appropriate safeguarding/reporting applicability and
deadline decision; backup route if the lead is unavailable. Denial: ordinary
queue, AI-only decision or a generic timer cannot substitute for that branch.
Required evidence: decision rationale, authority/recipient verification and an
unsent synthetic escalation payload. Statutory reporting requirements must be
established for the actual operator; none are invented here. Stop before any
real dispatch, harmful material collection or unapproved preservation.

## Scenario 4 Incorrect report and appeal

**Status: NOT RUN.** Input: fabricated CASE-04 alleges a rule breach, followed by
an invented contradictory fact and appeal. Expected: proportionate human
assessment, original decision preserved, reasoned reconsideration and an
appropriately separated reviewer where practicable. Denial: report count alone
cannot establish guilt; appeal cannot expose reporter identity/internal notes.
Required evidence: audit survives reconsideration, truthful outcome copy and any
approved reversible restoration. Stop if the original record is overwritten or
the same reviewer is falsely described as independent. No live sanction is made.

## Scenario 5 Account deletion and safety evidence

**Status: NOT RUN.** Input: synthetic account closure with one open and one closed
case; repeat for reporter and reported person. Expected: ordinary publication
and account permissions cease as appropriate; only specifically justified minimum
case evidence survives where lawful, with access, expiry and hold-release tests.
Denial: no automatic loss of required evidence, indefinite blanket hold or
disclosure to a replacement Guardian. Required evidence: rights-aware lifecycle,
expiry, restore/deletion reconciliation and safe explanation. Preserve DR-ZYRA-248
intentions without treating them as universal legal mandates. Stop while lawful
retention/hold decisions or exact isolated execution authority are missing.

## Scenario 6 Privacy complaint and rights request

**Status: NOT RUN.** Input: CASE-06 contains both an invented privacy complaint
and subject-access/erasure request. A payer then asks for SIM-SEEKER-A's records.
Expected: linked tracks, proportionate identity checks and separately determined
deadlines; payer status grants no subject-access authority. Reassignment never
resets receipt or a statutory clock. Required evidence: deadline rationale,
restricted disclosures, truthful acknowledgement and continuing updates.
For the UK complaint rule where applicable, acknowledgement is within 30 days;
this is neither a 30-day resolution deadline nor permission to delay urgent
safety triage. Determine rights-request clocks/extensions separately. Stop on
invented exemptions or automatic payer/Guardian disclosure.
[ICO complaints guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/how-to-deal-with-data-protection-complaints/).

## Scenario 7 Suspected personal data breach

**Status: NOT RUN.** Input: invented disclosure of CASE-07's placeholder record;
awareness fixture 9 October 2026 at 10:00 UTC. Expected: human assessment of whether
a breach occurred and the risk to people, recorded rationale and notification
decision. If UK GDPR notification applies, the 72-hour outer reference is
12 October at 10:00 UTC; act without undue delay rather than wait for that time.
Incomplete facts must not postpone a required initial report; supplementary
information can follow. High-risk notification to people has its own without-
undue-delay requirement. Denial: an unverified vulnerability is not automatically
declared a breach. Required evidence: UTC clock arithmetic, unsent draft,
safe-recipient checks and justified no-notification record where appropriate.
Stop before any real filing or actual affected-person contact.
[ICO breach guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/report-a-breach/personal-data-breach/personal-data-breaches-a-guide/).

## Scenario 8 Human lead unavailable

**Status: NOT RUN.** Input: invented urgent CASE-08 arrives while the appointed
lead is unavailable; primary assignment fails, then a duplicate delivery occurs.
Expected: an appointed, trained backup receives and accepts one logical handoff
in a later authorised rehearsal, without clock reset or lost priority. Denial:
absence cannot cause false reviewed/closed status, endless retries or disclosure
through a shared Guardian/payer contact. Required evidence: coverage decision,
delivery/acknowledgement, escalation failure handling and continuity audit. Stop
if no suitable backup exists; record the capacity gap for a Founder service-scope
decision. This plan does not appoint the Founder as permanent emergency cover.
