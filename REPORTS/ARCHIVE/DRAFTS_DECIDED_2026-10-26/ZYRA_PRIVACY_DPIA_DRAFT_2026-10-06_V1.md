## 1. Result and limits

This is a preparation packet, not a completed DPIA, privacy notice, legal opinion,
accepted risk assessment or release clearance. It covers the broader ZYRA member
service and its website/contact/marketing boundary, rather than merely identity
verification. It does not supersede an existing CURRENT_VERSION document.

The evidence supports preparing a full assessment now. Sensitive matching,
relationship-dependent access, identity/duplicate-person checks and potential
physical-safety consequences are relevant screening factors. The ICO describes
DPIA screening as an assessment of likely high risk; low user count is not an
automatic exemption. A final determination needs the actual processing and scope.

checked local phone installation. They expressly do **not** establish production

open on this evidence. No actual disclosure, lost report, exploited person or
incident count is inferred. [E1–E3, appendix A]

## 5. Lawful-basis and necessity questions — no selection made

For Article 9 processing, an Article 6 basis is needed **as well as** a valid
special-category condition and any required DPA 2018 requirements. Contract or
legitimate interests alone do not satisfy Article 9. Decide each purpose before
adoption; do not apply one global checkbox to everything.
[ICO special-category rules](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/lawful-basis/special-category-data/what-are-the-rules-on-special-category-data/)

1. **Account/service (P1/P2/P8/P11):** which fields are objectively necessary
   for the requested service? What separate basis supports security/fraud and
   financial retention? Would less public identity or less stored history work?
2. **Matching/religious/ethnic data (P3/P4):** exact collection, calculation,
   storage, paid disclosure and future learning are separate purposes. Is each
   necessary/proportionate? If using explicit consent, what exactly is agreed,
   by whom, for which recipient/role/stage, and how is withdrawal enforced?
   Private app sharing is not presumed “manifestly made public.”
3. **IDV/duplicate recognition (P6):** distinguish document/age checks, face
   matching, population duplicate search and profile-photo comparison. Does
   the selected provider actually support the approved scope? What suitable
   alternative/refusal path makes consent genuine? How are false matches,
   demographic accuracy, manual review, appeals and existing members treated?
   E6's “join or refuse” reasoning does not settle this. Current biometric-
   recognition guidance calls for genuine choice and a suitable alternative
   when relying on consent. Founder product approval cannot waive law.
   [ICO biometric lawfulness](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/lawful-basis/biometric-data-guidance-biometric-recognition/how-do-we-process-biometric-data-lawfully/)
4. **Guardian permissions (P7/P8):** separate a Seeker's deliberate relationship
   permission from the controller's lawful basis. Who controls withdrawal and
   past attempts? A Guardian, sibling or payer cannot consent for every other
   adult merely because they are connected or funding the membership.
5. **Safety (P10):** select/justify bases and any Article 9/10 conditions for
   reports, allegation handling, limited preservation, appeals and legally
   required disclosures. Never invent a universal indefinite “legal hold.”
6. **Diagnostics/learning (P12):** what necessary operational processing occurs
   locally today, versus the optional remote feed/learning being proposed?
   What objection/withdrawal path and aggregate-identifiability proof is needed?
7. **Marketing/enquiries (P13):** separate service replies, launch marketing,
   audience research and tracking. Consent-based electronic marketing needs its
   own evidence and opt-out unless a demonstrably applicable exception applies;
   enquiries/followers are not automatically a customer soft-opt-in list.
   [ICO electronic mail rules](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guidance-on-direct-marketing-using-electronic-mail/how-do-we-comply-with-the-pecr-electronic-mail-marketing-rules/)
8. **Automated outcomes:** assess actual matching/access, IDV refusal, moderation
   and any learning separately for significant effects and meaningful human
   involvement. Do not call a review button human review without an empowered,
   staffed process. ICO's DUAA summary describes changed automated-decision rules
   with safeguards and continued sensitive-data restrictions; reconcile the
   applicable commenced law and updated guidance before final drafting, rather
   than repeating a blanket historic Article 22 sentence.
   [ICO DUAA summary](https://ico.org.uk/about-the-ico/what-we-do/legislation-we-cover/data-use-and-access-act-2025/the-data-use-and-access-act-2025-duaa-summary-of-the-changes/data-protection/)

The general consent guidance also distinguishes consent as a service condition
from objectively necessary special-category processing. Neither “consent always
works” nor “membership conditions always invalidate it” is adopted here.
[ICO consent appropriateness](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/lawful-basis/consent/when-is-consent-appropriate/)

## 9. Rights and incident scenarios — tabletop proposals only

### Rights route

Before adoption, assign an accountable human and cover; verify existing public
privacy/support addresses, authentication proportionality, request recording,
applicable deadlines/escalation and vendor coordination. Do not add another
mailbox or change filters as part of this draft.

Tabletop fixtures should prove:

- An adult Seeker obtains their data without receiving another person's private
  replies, reporter identity, internal notes or IDV evidence by default.
- A Guardian/Family payer asks for a Seeker's data: relationship/payment alone
  grants no subject-access authority. Assess genuine authorised representation.
- A person corrects an erroneous DOB/identity/matching answer while respecting
  integrity constraints and preserving necessary correction history.
- A person withdraws profile/Guardian/history permission or sensitive-processing
  consent: distinguish access revocation, future calculation and justified record
  retention; make consequences clear, not a hidden automatic sanction.
- Account erasure removes publication and permitted personal data, explains any
  scoped justified exception, reaches provider/caches/backups and preserves no
  unnecessary connected-member data. Account closure and store cancellation are
  not assumed the same operation.
- A duplicate/refused IDV decision has a meaningful review and contest route;
  the process does not expose the other person's account or biometric material.
- An opt-out stops marketing across the approved channels without erasing the
  minimum suppression record and accidentally re-enrolling the person.

Prepare notices identifying the real controller, each purpose/basis, recipients,
transfers, retention/criteria, applicable rights, mandatory-data consequences and
relevant automated logic. Rights depend on the basis; do not promise every right
unconditionally or say accepting terms equals consent.
[ICO privacy information](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/individual-rights/the-right-to-be-informed/what-privacy-information-should-we-provide/)

### Incident route

Use invented descriptions only: foreign-photo URL accessible; newly appointed
Guardian can see ended chat; provider callback falsely marks verification;
mailbox takeover; old backup resurrects deleted profile; report disappears; or
push reveals a conversation on a shared locked phone. None occurred in this task.

Proposed procedure: record awareness time and safe facts; inform the accountable
human/cover; preserve minimal restricted evidence; classify confidentiality,
integrity and availability harm to people; seek exact authorised containment;
assess reporting and safe contact, involve processors as required; restore and
verify; document the incident/rationale and follow-up. Never email sensitive
details to a connected Guardian/payer as an automatic contact fallback.

The ICO's current guide requires notifiable breaches without undue delay and
within 72 hours of awareness, and high-risk notification to people without undue
delay; all breaches need a record. Incomplete facts do not justify waiting for a
complete investigation before a required report. This draft neither declares a
breach nor authorises a regulatory filing/provider message.
[ICO breach response](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/report-a-breach/personal-data-breach/personal-data-breaches-a-guide/)
