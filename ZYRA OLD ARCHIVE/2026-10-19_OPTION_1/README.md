# Old ZYRA Option 1 comparison — archived 19 October 2026

This is historical, nonbuildable evidence of the retired Founder comparison.
The Founder confirmed keeping the current purple ZYRA presentation and removing
the Option 1 comparison and white Option 2 study from active code.

The numbered text files preserve exact pre-edit comparison entry, callback,
destination and account-owned preference excerpts. `MANIFEST.json` records their
original file/line provenance and hashes, plus the complete immutable preimage
packet and historical introduction evidence. This folder is outside `SOURCE`,
is not imported or packaged by the app, and grants no runtime access.

The old comparison reused the ordinary member HomeShell and WaliHomeShell.
Those shared implementations and their real services remain in the current
source; they were not moved or deleted. This archive is not a separate working
app and must not be used as a replacement member implementation.

Historical governed documents and review packets remain preserved. Retirement
does not authorize real-member rollout, backend changes, or a release upload.
