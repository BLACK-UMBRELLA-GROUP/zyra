# Line 131 — LOCK 38 purchase wording

## Current state

**Built 24 Nov 2026 in the detached Founder preview.** Seeker One, Guardian
One, Compatibility Unlocks, Seeker Basic signup and the ZYRA Family price
sheet show the shared price or charge, renewal and cancellation wording from
one widget. The prices come from `ZyraMembershipCatalog` or the Family quote.
Sign-up puts the print and links above its pinned action and changes them with
the selected card; the other purchase screens put them below their action.
The Guardian One comparison says “Chat in a Journey, when your Seeker allows
it”. Its action opens “Before you pay”; the linked Seeker's first name is
shown and its tick is required before Continue to Apple becomes active.

This is sample presentation only. Terms, Privacy, Restore and Apple actions
show the exact Q205 preview notices. Compatibility Unlocks Continue keeps its
existing unavailable notice. None of these actions opens a URL, charges an
Apple ID, restores a purchase, grants access, sends a Family invite, or writes
to the server. The existing member app continues to require an App Store
product before making a price or trial claim in its purchase action. The
membership terms now describe Guardian One's possible 14-day offer only when
the App Store product carries it; ZYRA Family has no trial.
